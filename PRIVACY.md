# Privacy Policy — Archive This!

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

Archive This! is designed to be a minimal browser utility.

## Data collected by Archive This!

Archive This! does not collect, store, sell, share, or analyze personal information or browsing history.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, cookies, or remote telemetry.

## What happens when you click the extension

Archive This! uses Chrome's `activeTab` permission to read the URL of the tab you are actively viewing after you click the extension.

It removes common tracking parameters from that URL and navigates the tab to an Archive.today lookup through the `archive.is` domain. The page URL is therefore sent to that third-party service as necessary to perform the archive lookup.

Archive This! is not affiliated with Archive.today or archive.is. Their handling of requests is governed by their own policies and practices.

## Local storage

Archive This! does not store extension data locally.
